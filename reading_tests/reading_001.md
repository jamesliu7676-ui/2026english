# Reading 001: A Rainy Morning

- 主題：日常生活
- 來源：自編
- 字數：約 120 字
- 題型：主旨題、細節題、字彙題、推論題、是非判斷

## Passage

Mia usually walks to school, but this morning was different. When she opened the door, she saw dark clouds and heavy rain. She could not find her umbrella, so she called her father for help. Her father was busy, but he told her to wait for ten minutes. Later, he drove her to school. Mia was a little late, but her teacher smiled and said, "Many students are late today because of the rain."

## Questions

### 主旨題

1. What is the main idea of the passage?
   A. Mia likes walking to school.
   B. Mia had trouble getting to school because of the rain.
   C. Mia forgot to do her homework.
   D. Mia helped her father.

### 細節題

2. Why didn't Mia walk to school?
   A. She was sick.
   B. She lost her shoes.
   C. It was raining heavily.
   D. Her teacher called her.

### 字彙題

3. What does "busy" mean in the passage?
   A. having many things to do
   B. feeling very sad
   C. moving very slowly
   D. being very hungry

### 推論題

4. How did Mia probably feel when she arrived late?
   A. A little worried.
   B. Very angry.
   C. Very sleepy.
   D. Excited.

### 細節題

5. True or False: Mia's teacher was angry because she was late.

## Answer Key

1. B
2. C
3. A
4. A
5. False

## Notes

- 可延伸：請學生改寫故事結尾，或用 2 句英文描述 Mia 的早晨。
