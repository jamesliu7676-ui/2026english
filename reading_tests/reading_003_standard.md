## Passage A

Last month, Sunny High School noticed a problem in the cafeteria. Many students bought drinks every day, but most of them threw away plastic cups after lunch. The cleaning team collected more than 300 cups each week. Some students thought this was normal, but the school environment club disagreed.

The club started a "Bring Your Own Cup" campaign. At first, only a few students joined it. To encourage more students, the club put up posters near the cafeteria and gave small stickers to students who used their own cups. Two weeks later, the number of plastic cups dropped by almost half. The cafeteria also began to offer a small discount to students with reusable cups.

The campaign did not solve every problem, but it helped students see that a small daily habit could make a difference.

## Passage B

School Notice:

> Starting next Monday, students who bring a reusable cup to the cafeteria will receive a NT$5 discount on drinks. Please wash your cup before using it.
