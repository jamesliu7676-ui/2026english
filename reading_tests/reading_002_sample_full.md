# Reading 002: Choosing a Weekend Activity

- 主題：日常生活、活動選擇
- 來源：自編
- 字數：約 180 字
- 題型：主旨題、細節題、推論題、字彙題、代名詞指涉題、句意理解題、段落排序、多文本整合題、圖表閱讀題、混合題：選擇 + 簡答

## Passage A

Kevin and his classmates wanted to do something meaningful on Saturday. At first, they planned to watch a movie together. However, their teacher told them that the community center needed volunteers for a clean-up activity in the park. Kevin was not excited at first because he wanted to relax after a busy week. Later, he changed his mind when he learned that many young children played in the park every afternoon.

On Saturday morning, Kevin and his classmates picked up bottles, paper cups, and plastic bags. The work was tiring, but they finished before noon. When Kevin saw the cleaner park, he felt proud. He said, "I thought this would be boring, but it was actually worth it."

## Passage B

Message from Kevin to Amy:

> We are going to the park clean-up this Saturday. Please bring a hat and a bottle of water. The activity starts at 8:30 a.m. and ends at 11:30 a.m. We will meet at the school gate at 8:00.

## Chart

| Activity | Time | Place | Things to Bring |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Park clean-up | 8:30-11:30 a.m. | Green Park | hat, water |
| Movie | 2:00-4:00 p.m. | Star Theater | ticket |
| Basketball game | 4:30-6:00 p.m. | School gym | sports shoes |

### 句意理解題

### 多文本整合題
